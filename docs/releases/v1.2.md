# Pwint Thit POS 1.2

Purchases now provide a continuous workspace from supplier purchase through shipment, transportation, landed costing and goods receiving. Transportation can remain pending until its details are known. Reopening the same purchase restores progress from saved records. Remaining goods are included automatically when arranging a shipment. Multiple partial shipments remain linked, and completion requires all purchased quantities to be allocated and all active shipments to have posted receipts. Receiving updates inventory through immutable movements.

Optional sections expand with checkboxes: supplier contacts/trading terms, product barcode/origin/category/brand/description/stock alerts, purchase due date/invoice reference/notes/discount/tax, shipment arrival/notes, transportation journey details and extra fees, additional shipment costs, and optional receiving counts/notes/dates. Expiry stays visible and required for products that track it. Collapsing a section preserves entered or recorded values.

## Automatic IDs

New records retain UUID primary keys and receive readable business identifiers on save. Number counters are transaction safe, independent per type and Myanmar business year, and roll back with failed writes. Four-digit suffixes expand beyond 9999. Existing purchase/shipment/receipt/product/supplier/invoice/payment identifiers stay intact. Added reference fields supply sales-order, transportation, adjustment and issue IDs for existing records too; their dates and financial values stay intact.

| Record | Format |
| --- | --- |
| Product / SKU | PRD-2026-0001 |
| Supplier | SUP-2026-0001 |
| Purchase | PUR-2026-0001 |
| Shipment | SHP-2026-0001 |
| Transportation | TRN-2026-0001 |
| Goods receipt | GRN-2026-0001 |
| Sales order | SO-2026-0001 |
| Invoice | INV-2026-0001 |
| Payment | PAY-2026-0001 |
| Stock adjustment | ADJ-2026-0001 |
| Damage | DMG-2026-0001 |
| Missing / loss | LOSS-2026-0001 |

The year reflects the Myanmar business date at save time. Explicit legacy/import identifiers remain supported by the API; normal creation forms no longer request transaction IDs. Matching imported identifiers reserve their counter value to avoid collisions.

## Super Admin controls and intentional restrictions

Super Admin receives every registered backend permission. Staff grants remain enforced on every protected API.

| Data | Available controls | Accounting/security restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Products and suppliers | Create, view, edit, deactivate, archive | Used product base units cannot change; archiving retains history |
| Catalog records | Create, view, edit, deactivate | Referenced history is retained |
| Warehouses | Create, edit, archive inside shipment setup | Codes and existing stock/shipment links remain intact |
| Open shipments | Create, view, edit origin/destination/arrival/notes, update status, cancel | Closed/finalized shipments retain their costing and goods history |
| Transportation stages | Create, view, edit, remove with reason | Paid or finalized stages cannot be removed; deleted unpaid-stage values remain in audit history |
| Shipment expenses | Create, view, void and replace with reason | Paid/finalized costs and stored historical rates are protected |
| Posted purchases | View, reverse before downstream activity, recreate correction | No overwrite/delete of posted amounts/rates; active shipments, any payment allocation or supplier returns block simple reversal |
| Receipts and inventory | Receive, view history, controlled stock adjustments | Posted counts and batch costs are immutable; stock changes originate from movements |
| Sales, payments, returns and operating expenses | Existing permission-enforced sales/payment/return/refund/reversal controls | Completed records and consumed approvals stay auditable |
| Users, grants and audit | Existing account/grant management and audit trail | Owner-only grant administration and append-only audit history |
| Exchange rates | Add future quotes or an authorized custom transaction rate | Historical quotes are immutable |

Purchase reversal is recorded as an immutable event. Current payables exclude the reversed purchase; reports for dates before the reversal retain it. Purchase and currency reports include the original and equal opposite reversal on their respective event dates.

## Distribution follow-up: payments, warehouse pricing and fulfillment

The Payment centre brings supplier, customer and transportation payments together, with each entry linked to its purchase, invoice, cargo stage or separate shipment cost. Cargo debt remains separate from supplier debt. Cancelled shipment charges retain their payment history.

Warehouse prices supports independent carton and individual-unit prices at every warehouse. Customer special prices retain priority; warehouse prices override product defaults. Stock issues accept package quantities with server-side base-unit conversion. Shipments and sales now record collection/delivery and optional person/vehicle details, while historical records remain unchanged.

See `docs/distribution-payments-warehouse-pricing.md` for the controls and workflow. Validation includes the database integration suite, race tests/vet, frontend lint/build and 26 real-stack browser tests.

## Landed-cost warehouse selling prices

Product forms no longer ask for purchase or selling prices. Warehouse pricing selects a finalized shipment at the chosen warehouse and shows allocated purchase, cargo, other and landed costs per package and base unit. Enter a base-unit selling price or apply a markup on landed cost; retail and wholesale values remain separate. Both package/base-unit prices save atomically with an audited cost reference. Immutable costs and previous invoice prices are retained. Migration 000032 is additive.

## Transportation fee basis

Each transport stage supports a total shipment fee or a per-carton rate multiplied by the billed carton count. Carton counts are suggested from shipment packaging where known and can be adjusted to match the cargo bill. Exact server-side calculation preserves the rate/count and resulting total. Additional charges stay separate. Existing stages remain total-fee entries with unchanged amounts. Migration 000033 is additive.

## Arrival-based sellable quantities

Shipment costing no longer asks for a manually selected sellable quantity before arrival. Planning previews use expected goods. After arrival, actual received and damaged counts determine sellable quantity automatically; confirmation saves both counts with the immutable costing snapshot. Goods receiving prefills and reconciles those counts. Migration 000034 leaves older finalized records unchanged.

## Shipment quantity display

Shipment creation now shows available and entered quantities in both cartons and individual units, including loose-unit remainders. Both entry points use the same display and keep allocation quantities unchanged.

## Package-based cargo entry

Transportation now starts with a fee for one package: **Package Type**, **Package Quantity**, **Fee per Package**, and calculated **Total Cargo Fee**. Packaging can be carton, pack or another configured product unit. For example, 20,000 MMK per carton × 5 cartons = 100,000 MMK. Additional costs remain separate. Migration 000035 retains legacy fees unchanged and adds exact package-rate snapshots. The earlier total/per-carton form is superseded; existing total-only fees need an explicit package rate/count when edited.

**Expected Base Quantity** and shipment creation show package quantities and their equivalent individual stock counts, including loose units. Verified with backend integration tests, build/lint/race checks and all 28 Chrome/Safari browser tests.

## Optional POS credit sales

The POS panel now has an unchecked **Sell on Credit** checkbox. Immediate-payment sales require full payment and hide the credit due date. Selecting credit allows zero or partial payment, shows the due date, and retains customer and credit-limit validation. A blank payment means exact payment for an immediate sale and zero paid for a credit sale. The server rejects unpaid balances unless credit is explicitly selected; the choice is part of the saved quote/invoice snapshot. Migration 000036 preserves existing invoices and their retry path.

## Warehouse shipments, Business data and assistant demo

Warehouse legs now support one source and destination, with full or partial dispatch, in-transit stock, confirmed receiving, damaged/missing reconciliation and controlled return-to-source cancellation. Cargo stages and additional expenses use existing shipment/payment records. Destination batches inherit source cost and expiry, add allocated cargo/additional costs, and keep immutable links to the source. Destination pricing and FIFO sales use the new landed cost; supplier returns retain the original purchase lineage. Migration 000037 is additive and preserves existing records.

Business data brings sales, inventory, stock movements and payments into one filtered tabular page. Inventory separates inbound/outbound sellable stock; the new Payment Ledger report links posted payments to their transactions. There are now 15 reports, all permission enforced. The AI assistant page is an explicitly labelled sample-response demo, with no API connection or business-data access, as requested.
